TEMPO now estimates demand for each substation and calculates PV and wind profiles for your technologies.

## Added

- Substation demand estimation in the Study Area tool, anywhere in the world. It uses a 1 km consumption map, national statistics, mine sites and OpenStreetMap land use. You can edit each substation before import.
- Demand cells layer on the map, coloured by density or total.
- PV and wind profiles from Open-Meteo ERA5 weather, with a map, charts and a per-point table. PV tilt and azimuth can be optimised.
- Sunshine and wind overlays on the map.
- Components installer in Settings to install and remove engines, solvers, services and tools.
- Chronological time scheme for OSeMOSYS windows of up to 31 days.

## Changed

- Results are always shown in MW. A saved kW model needs its Model unit set on the Run page.
- `demand-venv` replaces `demandlib-venv`.
- Overpass queries go through the backend.

## Fixed

- Location names are unique, and duplicates in older models are repaired on load.
- Technologies missing from a model are added when it loads.
