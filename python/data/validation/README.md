# Validation data

## `cen_withdrawals_<year>.csv` — CEN energy withdrawals per barra (Chile, SEN)

Ground truth for the per-substation demand estimate: annual energy withdrawals
(*retiros*) per SEN bus (*barra*), from the Coordinador Eléctrico Nacional (CEN).
Generated by `scripts/fetch_cen_withdrawals.py` — regenerate, don't hand-edit.

**Source.** CEN, *Antecedentes de cálculo para las Transferencias Económicas*, monthly
**definitive** publication (first definitive, not later re-liquidations RxxD), zip
`01-Resultados*.zip` → `01 Resultados/01 Balance de Energía/02 Balance Físico/`
`REVISION_{NORTE,NORTE_DX,SUR,SUR_DX}_YYMM.xls`, sheet *Balance por Barra*.
Year index: <https://www.coordinador.cl/mercados/documentos/transferencias-economicas/antecedentes-de-calculo-para-las-transferencias-economicas/2024-antecedentes-de-calculo-para-las-transferencias-economicas/>
(e.g. January 2024: `https://www.coordinador.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/01-Resultados-1.zip`).
The zips are ~170 MB/month; the script range-reads only the four balance workbooks
(~1.5 MB/month).

**Coordinates.** CEN Infotécnica API, substation records
(`https://api-infotecnica.coordinador.cl/v1/subestaciones/` and
`/v1/subestaciones/extended/<id>/` → `latitud`, `longitud`). The balance has no
coordinates and no Infotécnica id, so barras are matched to substations **by name**
(balance names are truncated to 14 characters and abbreviated; exact → unique prefix →
fuzzy ≥ 0.88). `infotecnica_subestacion` records the match so it can be audited; blank
lat/lon = no match, or `ambiguous: …` when several same-name substations lie > 20 km
apart (e.g. SAN JOAQUIN in Santiago and La Serena). Spot-check before trusting a match.
2024: 476 / 554 barras (87 % of energy) have coordinates.

**Retrieved:** 2026-11-06.

authorising third parties; downloading public information for personal or **academic**
use and citing it **with clear attribution** are allowed; commercial resale/redistribution,

The CSV is a derived aggregate for academic validation; cite "Coordinador Eléctrico
Nacional, Balance Físico de Energía <year>". Run the fetch script sparingly (it makes
~50 range requests to coordinador.cl and ~1 300 + one-per-match requests to the
Infotécnica API) or ask CEN for authorisation.

**Columns.** `barra` (CEN balance name, `NAME____kV`), `barra_id` (CEN balance barra
number), `kv` (from the name suffix; `013` = 13.2/13.8 kV class), `annual_gwh` =
`regulated_gwh` + `free_gwh`, `regulated_gwh` (meter type R: regulated clients via
distribution companies), `free_gwh` (L: free clients on transmission, L_D: free clients
in distribution), `ssaa_gwh` (L_S: substation auxiliaries, not in `annual_gwh`),
`lat`, `lon`, `infotecnica_subestacion`, `source_url`. Energy = *Medida 3* (after
substitution and bar balancing), kWh in the source, sign flipped so withdrawals are
positive. Net negative values can occur where a meter nets embedded generation.

**Caveats.**
- These are **metered withdrawals at the CEN balance point**, not end-use consumption:
  distribution losses downstream of the barra are included in regulated withdrawals;
  self-generation and off-grid consumption (e.g. mines with own plants) are not.
- One barra can feed several distribution substations (and several OSM substations); some
  distribution-level barras (`*_DX` balances, 13.2/23 kV) are fed through *neteo* meters
  from a transmission barra — withdrawals are attributed where the client meter sits.
- Regulated withdrawals are split by supplier contract (EMENA/B/C…) in the source; they
  are summed here.
- Only the SEN (no Aysén / Magallanes medium systems).
- 2024 total: 79 732 GWh (regulated 30 990, free 48 742); monthly 6 250–6 980 GWh.
  For comparison, CEN reported about 77 300 GWh of withdrawals for 2023.
- Workbook names drift between months (`REVISION_NORTE_2401.xls`,
  `REVISION_RES_NORTE_2405.xls`, `REVISION_TFE_RES_NORTE_2411_DX.xls`). The script
  warns if a month does not yield exactly four workbooks.
